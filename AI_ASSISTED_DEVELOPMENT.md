# AI-Assisted Development

AI tools may assist with drafting code, tests, documentation, and review prompts in this project.
They are not a source of authority for product claims, safety decisions, or acceptance evidence.

Humans remain responsible for defining requirements, reviewing changes, protecting private data,
running tests, and deciding whether hardware or call-application gates have passed. Generated text
and code must be checked against the repository, documentation, and observed results before merge.

For this prototype, deterministic tests support design decisions; they do not establish real-device
audio isolation, compatibility with a third-party call application, translation fitness, or user
safety. Those assertions require the documented operator evidence.
