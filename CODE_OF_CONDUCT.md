# Code of Conduct

We want a constructive, respectful project space. Be considerate, assume good intent, and focus
technical criticism on ideas, evidence, and code rather than people.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private data,
and deliberately misleading security, privacy, or compatibility claims.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and
users. Report concerns privately through the contact path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), including
the relevant context and any immediate safety concern.
